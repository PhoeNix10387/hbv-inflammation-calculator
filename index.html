Reading the output

Positive means the predicted probability ≥ the selected threshold; Negative means it is below that threshold. The development-derived Youden threshold is the default. This is a statistical classification, not a clinical action recommendation.

The three outcomes were modelled independently. Their probabilities need not be ordered and do not form a four-class probability distribution.

Target sensitivity and specificity use saved development-stage predictions. The displayed rates are operating-point references, not independent validation estimates or guarantees for new patients. Changing the threshold changes classification, not probability.

All prediction calculations run in your browser. Predictor values are not uploaded, stored or sent to an analytics service. The static website host serves the page and model files.
